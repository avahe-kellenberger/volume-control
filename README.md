# Volume Control

A RuneLite plugin for adjusting the volume of individual sound effects.

1. Enable **Volume Control** in RuneLite and open its sidebar panel.
2. Enter a **Sound Name** (any label you'd like) and the numeric **Sound ID** you want to change.
3. Choose **Sound** for a regular sound effect or **Area** for an area sound.
4. Set the **Volume** from **0** (muted) to **127** (maximum).
5. Click **Play** to preview the sound, then **Submit** to save.

For area sounds, leave **Positional audio** checked to make the sound quieter with distance.
Uncheck it to keep the volume constant at any distance.

Under **Saved Sounds**, use **Play** to preview, **Edit** to change a sound (then click **Submit**), or **Delete** to remove its custom settings. Saved settings are kept between sessions.

Use **Sort by** to order saved sounds by name (A to Z or Z to A), when they were added (oldest or newest first), or type (Area or Sound first, then by name). Your sort preference is kept between sessions. Editing a sound preserves its added order; existing sounds use their saved order.

Enable **Hide side panel button** in Volume Control's RuneLite settings to hide its sidebar icon. Custom sound volumes still apply while the icon is hidden. Disable the setting to show the icon again; changes take effect immediately.
